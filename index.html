Customer care

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful information about choosing your style, placing an order and caring for your pieces.

How do I choose the right size?

Use the size guide as a starting point and check the measurements listed on each product. If you are between sizes, contact customer care with your measurements.

Can I request a different colour or size?

Availability and customisation depend on the design. Contact RAVOSH before placing your order to confirm what is possible.

How can I track my order?

Order tracking must be connected to the store's order management or courier service. Until then, contact customer care with your order reference.

What is the return and exchange policy?

Publish your confirmed return window, eligibility, condition requirements and exchange process here before accepting live orders.

Which payment methods are available?

A little about us

Style is personal.
Make it yours.

At RAVOSH, we believe the best style doesn't shout. It speaks in thoughtful details, beautiful fabrics and the confidence to be completely yourself. Every piece is selected to make getting dressed feel a little more special.

Founded in 2026, RAVOSH was born from a simple idea: that elegance should feel effortless. We work with independent ateliers and fabric mills to create limited-run pieces that balance modern silhouettes with timeless craft.

Our collections are designed to be worn, loved and lived in — not just for special occasions, but for the everyday moments that deserve a little beauty.
